## 7. Design reference findings (summary — full detail in [design-direction.md](../design-direction.md))
ai.gov.rw was inspected live in the browser at 1440 px, the pane's native ~800 px, and 375 px mobile emulation, including DOM and CSS analysis. Key patterns:
1. A full-bleed **hero slideshow with three captioned chapters** ("01 · …", "02 · …", "03 · …"), segmented progress bar, `01 / 03` counter and prev/next arrows. This maps directly to *01 Faith & Worship / 02 Community & Discipleship / 03 Global Mission*.
2. **Every section header** has an uppercase kicker with a short rule, a large H2, a lead paragraph, and a page-level `01 / 04` index on the right.
3. A **transparent header** turns into a frosted white bar on scroll. Below 1024 px a burger opens a full-screen drawer.
4. Hairline-bordered **grid cells** instead of drop-shadow cards; numbered **row lists** with sliding arrows; two large **image "pathway" cards**.
5. Inner pages: a 7/5 **page hero** plus a **sticky numbered table of contents** (01–07) beside the content blocks.
6. Restrained motion: a 26 px fade-up reveal, clip reveals, slow image zoom on hover, fill-from-bottom button hover, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support.

**Browser limitation, stated honestly:** desktop and hero screenshots were captured. Midway through, the app pane was hidden, so later screenshots (mid-page sections at desktop and the whole mobile view) could not render. Those sections were documented from the live DOM and computed CSS instead. Re-take the visual screenshots during Phase 3.
